THE FILM

Toronto and Ontario road-trip footage across the skyline, markets, and highway drives.

Shot on Fujifilm X-S20 (F-Log2) with two iPhones and a Canon digicam. Only a trailer and a single graded clip are finished so far — the full travel cut isn't edited yet.

THE CLOCK DRIFT

The camera thought it was June 2024.

The Fuji's internal clock had drifted +790 days, 15:17:58, so every clip self-reported a date two years early. The real dates were recovered by matching the footage against GPS-tagged iPhone photos from the same trip — Stackt Market, the CN Tower, the MASA taco truck. The clips are now sorted into location folders because of it.
