the story

One afternoon, held longer than it should have been. Two people in a field at the edge of the city, then at the water, then apart — the same sun behind them each time, lower every time. There is no dialogue. What passes between them happens in the space they keep leaving and closing.

Medium dictates narrative structure.

Written for COMPLIT 382 — on what Guadagnino and Ivory gave up when they cut the novel's epilogue, and what silence, music and a held camera did in its place.
