SYNOPSIS

A multi-shooter rave promo for OSTAR × House Bl3nd.

Shot on Sony FX3 (S-Log3) with camcorder and stills coverage. The final cut is the party — Scene 2 — only: the planned parking-lot scene (Scene 1) was cut entirely. The hero film, a watermarked cut, two breakdown reels, and a coloring carousel all ship from this project.
